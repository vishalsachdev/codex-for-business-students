NovaBrew Coffee Co.

Business Model Canvas
"Your perfect cup, delivered." — Austin, TX • Founded 2022
🤝 Key Partners
  • 47 independent specialty roasters (Onyx, Verve, Ruby, Heart, etc.)
  • Direct-trade coffee farmers across 5 regions
  • USPS (First Class & Priority Mail shipping)
  • Shopify (subscription platform infrastructure)
  • Compostable packaging suppliers
  • Carbon offset provider (carbon-neutral shipping)
  • Roast Ventures & angel investors (seed funding)
⚙️ Key Activities
  • Coffee curation & roaster sourcing
  • Monthly subscription fulfillment (roast → ship within 48hrs)
  • Recommendation engine & personalization (v1 — needs work)
  • Customer acquisition via paid & organic channels
  • Brand & content marketing
  • Retention & churn reduction initiatives
🏗️ Key Resources
  • Roaster partner network (47 curated relationships)
  • Proprietary subscription & inventory platform
  • Maya's Q Grader certification & coffee expertise
  • Brand identity & customer trust
  • Subscriber data & behavioral insights
  • 12 FTEs (founders + ops + marketing + support)
  • $800K seed funding (~8 months runway)
💎 Value Propositions
  • Discovery: Every bag is a chance to try exceptional specialty coffee from independent roasters
  • Freshness: Ships within 48 hours of roasting — not off a warehouse shelf
  • Convenience: Curated coffee delivered to your door monthly
  • Quality: Only specialty-grade beans from vetted roaster partners
  • Sustainability: Direct trade, compostable packaging, carbon-neutral shipping
  • Aspiration: "Coffee personality" — a subscription that knows you (not yet delivered)
💛 Customer Relationships
  • Self-service subscription management
  • 2 dedicated customer support reps
  • Warm, conversational brand voice ("barista who remembers your order")
  • Tasting notes & origin story cards (Enthusiast+)
  • Retention campaigns (loyalty points, referral bonuses, seasonal blends)
  • Gap: No feedback loop or rating mechanism
📦 Channels
  • Direct website (Shopify-powered)
  • Paid digital (social ads, search — CAC trending up)
  • Organic social & content marketing
  • Referral program
  • Gift subscriptions & e-gift cards
  • Email marketing & welcome series
  • USPS delivery (First Class & Priority)
👥 Customer Segments
  • Explorer: Casual drinker (~2 cups/day), wants to try good coffee without commitment ($16/mo)
  • Enthusiast: Regular drinker (2–3 cups/day), appreciates quality & variety ($24/mo)
  • Connoisseur: Serious home brewer, wants to compare origins & nuance ($36/mo)
  • Gift buyers: 3- or 6-month gift subscriptions, gift boxes
  • One-time buyers: Non-subscribers purchasing individual bags or bundles
💰 Cost Structure
  • ~$45K/mo burn rate (salaries, COGS, marketing, ops)
  • COGS: Coffee beans, roasting fees, compostable packaging
  • Shipping: USPS First Class & Priority (free to subscriber)
  • Salaries: 12 FTEs (3 founders + 9 staff)
  • Marketing & customer acquisition (paid channels trending higher)
  • Platform: Shopify subscription, hosting, tools
  • Warehouse & fulfillment operations
💵 Revenue Streams
  • Subscriptions (primary): $16–$36/mo × 8,200 active subscribers (~$184K/mo blended)
  • Avg. revenue/subscriber: $22.40/mo
  • One-time coffee purchases ($18–$42)
  • Gift subscriptions & gift boxes ($35–$204)
  • Brewing equipment add-ons (12–62% margins)
  • E-gift cards ($25–$100)
  • Key metric: LTV $187 (down from $320) · CAC $38 · Churn ~12%
